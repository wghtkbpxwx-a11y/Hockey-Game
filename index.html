HOW TO WIN UGLY

BIG HITS — line a puck carrier up at speed and hit L. They go down, the puck comes loose, and your super meter fills.
ONE-TIMERS — pass across the slot and tap shoot just before the puck arrives for a rocket you can't get off any other way.
SUPER SHOT — when the meter under your score is full, hold shoot to full charge and hold turbo, then let go. Goalies hate it.
ON FIRE — score twice in a row with the same skater and he catches fire: unlimited turbo, more speed, harder shots. It ends when the other side scores.
